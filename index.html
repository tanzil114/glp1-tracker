The GLP-1 tracker that actually tracks your food.

Log your shots, your protein, your weight and how you feel. Everything stays on your phone.

What it does

  • Every shot: dose, date and injection site, with rotation suggestions
  • A real food database with barcode scanning that works offline
  • Protein first, because protecting muscle is what matters most on these medications
  • Weight with a smoothed trend line, so one bad morning is not a story
  • Side effects plotted against your dose history
  • Vial and compounded dosing in syringe units or milligrams

Important

This app is a log and a companion for people already prescribed GLP-1 medication by a doctor. It does not prescribe, recommend or adjust doses, and it cannot assess symptoms. Your prescriber decides your dose. Always talk to your doctor about your treatment.

Nutrition data from USDA FoodData Central. Barcode data from Open Food Facts (ODbL).
