Managing your subscription

Subscriptions are handled by Apple, not by us. To view, change or cancel: open the Settings app, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, then choose GLP-1 Tracker. You can also open apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.

Cancelling stops the next renewal. You keep premium until the period you already paid for ends.

The free trial

The annual plan starts with 7 days free. We send you a reminder 2 days before it ends, so you are never surprised by a charge. Cancel any time during the trial and you pay nothing.

Common questions

Do I need an account? No. There is no sign-up and no login, ever.

Does it work offline? Yes. Food search, barcode lookup for bundled products, and all logging work with no signal.

I use a vial and syringe, not a pen. Set your concentration once in the medication course, then log in syringe units or milligrams. The app converts between them live.

Can I get my data out? Everything is stored on your device. Export is on our list.

A food is missing or has wrong values. Tell us the product and barcode and we will look at it. Barcode data comes from Open Food Facts, which anyone can correct.

How do I delete everything? Delete the app. There is no copy anywhere else.
