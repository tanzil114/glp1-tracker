Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 August 2026

The short version

Everything you log stays on your phone. We do not have a server, we do not have your data, and

there is no account to create.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. There is no sign-up, no email address, and no user ID.

What you enter, and where it lives

Your medication, doses, injection sites, weight, food entries and side effects are stored in a

database file on your device. They are not transmitted to us. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Network requests the app makes

1. Barcode lookups. When you scan a barcode that is not in the bundled food database, the app

sends ONLY that barcode number to Open Food Facts to retrieve product nutrition. No health

data, no identifier, and no other information is sent.

2. Nothing else. The app makes no other network requests.

Analytics and advertising

None. There is no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no attribution SDK in this app.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple or Google. We receive anonymous purchase status only. We

never see your payment details.

Health platform data

If you grant permission, the app can read and write weight and nutrition to Apple Health or

Health Connect. This stays between the app and the platform on your device.

Children

Not directed at children. The app is rated 17+ because it concerns prescription medication.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct or delete. You can

erase everything from Settings, or by deleting the app.

Changes

Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
